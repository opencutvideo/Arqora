ARQORA ("we", "us", "our") operates the ARQORA architecture project operating system (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Service.

By using ARQORA, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Data we collect

Account information

  • Name and email — used to identify you and send notifications.
  • Workspace details — your studio name, logo, currency, and timezone preferences.
  • Authentication data — securely hashed passwords. We never store plain-text passwords.

Project data

  • Project information — names, codes, descriptions, client details, site addresses, and GPS coordinates.
  • Documents and drawings — files you upload, including metadata like categories and revision numbers.
  • Site data — site diaries, photos, inspection records, and progress updates.
  • Financial data — budget items, BOQ/RAB entries, change orders, and material costs.

Usage data

  • Activity logs — audit trail of actions (create, update, delete) for accountability.
  • Device and browser — browser type, screen size, and theme preference for compatibility.

How we use your data

  • To provide the Service — display your projects, tasks, documents, and enable collaboration.
  • To send notifications — task assignments, approvals, and sync status updates.
  • To improve the platform — understand which features are used and fix issues.
  • To ensure security — audit logs help detect unauthorized access and track changes.
  • For offline functionality — data is cached locally so you can work without a connection.

We do not use your data to train AI models, sell to third parties, or for advertising.

Data storage

Local storage

ARQORA is an offline-first application. Your data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage. This allows full functionality without an internet connection.

Cloud storage

When online, data syncs to our secure cloud infrastructure. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security). Data at rest is encrypted at the database level.

Note: In the current demo build, data is stored locally in your browser only. Clearing your browser data will remove all project information. Cloud sync is available in the production deployment.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share data only in these cases:

  • Within your workspace — team members in the same workspace can see shared project data based on their role.
  • Service providers — hosting and database providers who help operate the Service, under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request a copy of your data at any time.
  • Correction — update your profile, project, or workspace information.
  • Deletion — delete projects, documents, or your entire workspace.
  • Export — export your data as CSV or PDF from the Reports page.
  • Opt-out — disable notifications or certain features at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app Settings page or contact us at the email listed below.

Cookies and local storage

ARQORA uses browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to:

  • Store your theme preference (light, dark, or system).
  • Cache project data for offline use.
  • Keep you signed in during your session.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms.
  • Row-level security policies restrict data access to authorized users.
  • Audit logs track all create, update, and delete actions.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please contact us responsibly.

Data retention

  • Active data — kept as long as your workspace is active.
  • Deleted projects — soft-deleted and recoverable for 30 days, then permanently removed.
  • Audit logs — retained for 12 months for accountability.
  • Account closure — all data is deleted within 30 days of account closure.

Children's privacy

ARQORA is designed for professional architecture and construction studios. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a minor has provided data, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the application or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
