What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. Local storage is a similar browser feature that allows web applications to store data locally in your browser.

ARQORA primarily uses local storage (not traditional cookies) to provide offline-first functionality and remember your preferences.

Good news: ARQORA does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. We don't track you across other websites.

How we use browser storage

We use browser storage for the following purposes:

  • Offline functionality — cache project data so the app works without an internet connection.
  • Theme preference — remember whether you prefer light, dark, or system theme.
  • Session management — keep you signed in during your session.
  • Performance — store frequently accessed data locally to reduce server requests.

All storage is first-party — set and read only by ARQORA. No third party has access to this data.

Third-party cookies

ARQORA does not use third-party cookies or tracking technologies. We do not embed:

  • Advertising networks (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • Analytics trackers (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Social media widgets or share buttons that track you
  • Third-party chat widgets or support trackers

The only external resources we load are Google Fonts (for typography) and our favicon. These do not set tracking cookies.

Managing and clearing storage

You can control browser storage in several ways:

In-app

  • Use Settings → Reset data to clear all local data and reset the workspace.
  • Signing out clears your session token.

In your browser

  • Chrome/Edge — Settings → Privacy → Site settings → arqora.app
  • Firefox — Settings → Privacy → Cookies & Site Data → Manage Data
  • Safari — Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Note: Clearing local storage will remove all offline-cached project data. You will need to re-sync from the server when you next go online.

Changes to this policy

If we introduce new cookies or storage mechanisms in the future, we will update this policy and notify you in the application. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
