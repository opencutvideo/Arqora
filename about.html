About ARQORA

Architecture's calm command center.

ARQORA is a project operating system built for architecture and construction studios — connecting design, documents, site, and team in one workspace that works online or off.

Our mission

Architecture studios juggle dozens of tools — one for drawings, another for tasks, a spreadsheet for the budget, a chat app for the site. Information gets lost. Deadlines slip. Nobody has the full picture.

We built ARQORA to bring every part of a project into one calm, connected workspace. From the first concept sketch to the final handover, your team sees the same information — in the office or in the field, online or offline.

⚙

Clarity over chaos

Every project has a single source of truth. No more hunting through email threads or stale spreadsheets.

☁

Offline-first

Works on a construction site with no signal. Your data syncs the moment you reconnect.

⚙

Built for the field

Site diaries, photo logs, inspections, and RFIs — designed for a phone in a hard hat, not just a desk.

🔗

One connected workspace

Drawings, tasks, budget, BOQ, approvals, and discussions — linked, not siloed.

What we believe

  • Tools should reduce stress, not add to it. A clean interface and calm workflow matter as much as features.
  • The site and the studio deserve the same tool. Architects and builders shouldn't work in separate worlds.
  • Your data is yours. We don't lock you in. Export anytime, own your information.
  • Software should work offline. Construction sites have bad signal. Your tools should still work.

The platform

ARQORA covers the full project lifecycle:

  • Project management — brief, planning, design, drawings, documents
  • Construction — site diary, photos, progress tracking, inspections
  • Financial — budget, BOQ / RAB, change orders, materials
  • Collaboration — tasks, issues, RFIs, approvals, discussions, calendar
  • Reporting — weekly reports, construction progress, budget summaries
